Combat Classifications

Roles

Every Vanguard specializes in a major combat role, but their subclass determines how they fulfill that purpose on the battlefield.

The Three Pillars

Major Roles

Every character belongs to one of three major classifications: Vanguard, Striker, or Catalyst. Subclasses further define their preferred combat style, responsibilities, and strengths.

01
Major Role

Vanguard

Vanguards define and control the frontline. They absorb pressure, disrupt enemy advances, protect valuable space, and force opponents to fight on unfavorable terms.

Vanguard Subclasses
Vanguard

Guardian

Defensive specialists focused on keeping teammates alive through protection, mitigation, interception, and direct frontline presence.

Vanguard

Warden

Territory specialists who control important areas through structures, persistent effects, hazards, or other forms of battlefield denial.

Vanguard

Bludgeon

Aggressive Vanguards who create space through direct pressure, disruption, displacement, and sheer physical threat.

02
Major Role

Striker

Strikers are the primary offensive force of a team. Their kits are designed around securing eliminations, pressuring priority targets, and converting openings into damage.

Striker Subclasses
Striker

Brawler

Close-range combatants who thrive during prolonged engagements and maintain offensive pressure directly inside the enemy formation.

Striker

Marksman

Precision-focused ranged attackers who reward aim, target selection, and maintaining favorable sightlines.

Striker

Hunter

Target-focused killers built to track, isolate, chase, or punish vulnerable enemies.

Striker

Ravager

Highly aggressive damage dealers who use mobility, rapid pressure, or explosive combat patterns to overwhelm enemies.

Striker

Disruptor

Offensive specialists whose damage is paired with unusual interference, debuffs, battlefield manipulation, or mechanics that destabilize enemy plans.

03
Major Role

Catalyst

Catalysts amplify the effectiveness of their team. Their influence comes through healing, enabling allies, controlling engagements, manipulating positioning, or supplying specialized utility.

Catalyst Subclasses
Catalyst

Lifeline

Sustain specialists focused on restoring, preserving, or reinforcing allied health throughout an engagement.

Catalyst

Playmaker

Catalysts who create opportunities for their team through setup, tempo control, initiation, and powerful coordinated interactions.

Catalyst

Utility

Specialists built around unconventional tools, buffs, information, resources, or situational advantages that expand what their team can do.

Catalyst

Controller

Crowd-control specialists who manipulate enemy movement, positioning, actions, or access to important areas of the fight.
