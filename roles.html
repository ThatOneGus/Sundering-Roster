Roles

Every character specializes in a major combat role, but their subclass determines how they fulfill that purpose on the battlefield.

The Three Pillars

Major Roles

Every character belongs to one of three major classifications: Vanguard, Striker, or Catalyst. Subclasses further define their preferred combat style, responsibilities, and strengths.

01
Major Role

Vanguard

Vanguards define and control the frontline. They absorb pressure, disrupt enemy advances, protect valuable space, and force opponents to fight on unfavorable terms.

02
Major Role

Striker

Strikers are the primary offensive force of a team. Their kits are designed around securing eliminations, pressuring priority targets, and converting openings into damage.

Striker Subclasses
Striker

Brawler

Close-range combatants who thrive in prolonged engagements and maintain offensive pressure directly on the frontline.

Striker

Marksman

Precision-focused ranged attackers who reward aim, target selection, and maintaining favorable sightlines.

Striker

Hunter

Mobile killers built to dive, flank, isolate, chase, and eliminate vulnerable targets before escaping or repositioning.

Striker

Ravager

Highly aggressive damage dealers who use explosive pressure, relentless attacks, or powerful combat loops to overwhelm enemies.

Striker

Disruptor

Offensive specialists whose damage comes alongside unusual interference, debuffs, manipulation, or mechanics that destabilize enemy plans.

Major Role

Catalyst

Catalysts amplify the effectiveness of their team. Their influence comes through healing, enabling allies, controlling engagements, manipulating positioning, or providing specialized utility.
